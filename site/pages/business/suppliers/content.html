Who should list

If businesses buy it, it belongs here.

Packaging

Bottles, sacks, cartons, labels and wraps for food processors and manufacturers.

Machinery

Processing, milling, pressing and packing machines, new or refurbished.

Industrial equipment

Generators, storage, handling equipment and spare parts.

Manufacturing inputs

Chemicals, additives, fabrics and components that go into finished goods.

Agricultural inputs

Seeds, seedlings, fertiliser, feed and farm tools for producers.

Other commercial products

Distributors, wholesalers, importers and exporters are welcome too.

What you can do

Business terms on every listing.

  • Units and quantities

    Sell by the carton, drum, tonne or piece, and show how much you have.

  • Minimum orders

    Set the smallest order you'll take so enquiries fit your business.

  • Negotiable pricing

    Show a unit price as a starting point and settle the final figure in chat.

  • Specifications

    Add grade, size, material or any other detail buyers need to compare.

  • Capacity and availability

    Tell buyers how much you can produce and when it's available.

  • Procurement requests

    Send offers on requests from businesses that need what you supply.
