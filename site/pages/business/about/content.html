About Markt Business

Commerce between businesses, on the platform you already know.

Markt started as a place for people to discover and buy from local sellers. Many of those sellers — and many of the businesses behind the products — also need to buy from each other: a garri maker needs cassava, a juice producer needs bottles, a farm needs seed and equipment.

Markt Business extends Markt to those transactions. It connects producers, manufacturers and suppliers so they can find each other, share what they need, agree terms and complete the deal — without installing another app or managing another account.

We're starting with the foundations: business profiles, business listings, procurement requests, conversations and orders. We'll add more as businesses tell us what they need.
