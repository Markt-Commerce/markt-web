How it works

From registration to a completed deal.

Eight steps, all inside the Markt app and web app you already use.

  1. 1

    Create a business account

    Sign in with your Markt account, or create one. There's no separate business login: the same account works for everyday shopping and for your business.

  2. 2

    Set up your business profile

    Add your business name, the kind of business you are (producer, manufacturer, supplier, distributor and more — pick all that apply), your industry, location and contact details. Your account then switches into business mode.

  3. 3

    Discover businesses or list your products

    Buyers search business listings and profiles by product, type, industry and location. Sellers list products with business terms: unit, available quantity, minimum order, price per unit or negotiable, capacity and specifications.

  4. 4

    Communicate requirements

    Message a supplier about a listing, or post a procurement request with the product, quantity, specifications, delivery location and date. Suppliers respond with offers stating price and how much they can supply.

  5. 5

    Agree on specifications, quantity and pricing

    Negotiate in Markt chat. Conversations stay linked to the listing or request, so the details you agree are easy to find later.

  6. 6

    Create or confirm an order

    Accept the offer that works for you and place the order. A request or conversation never turns into an order on its own.

  7. 7

    Coordinate payment and delivery

    Pay through Markt checkout. Use Markt delivery where it covers your route and load; for larger bulk loads, agree transport with your supplier.

  8. 8

    Complete the transaction

    Track the order to delivery from your business dashboard. When you need more, the supplier is a message away.
