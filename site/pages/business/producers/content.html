Why join Markt?

Sell your capacity, not just your stock.

Find more buyers

Manufacturers and businesses search Markt for suppliers by product, location and quantity. Your business profile puts you in those results.

Showcase your products

List what you produce with photos, the unit you sell in, how much is available, your minimum order and your production capacity.

Manage supply enquiries

Buyers message you about a specific product. Each conversation stays linked to it, so you always know what's being discussed.

Receive procurement requests

See what businesses need — product, quantity, specifications, delivery location — and send an offer for the part you can supply.

Coordinate transactions

Agree price and terms in chat, then take the order through Markt and manage it alongside your other orders.

Set your own terms

Fix a price per unit or mark it negotiable. Set the smallest order you'll accept so you only hear from buyers that fit.

Getting started

How producer registration works.

  1. 01

    Sign in or create a Markt account

    Use the account you already shop or sell with. There is no separate business login.

  2. 02

    Add your business profile

    Business name, what kind of producer you are, your industry and where you're based.

  3. 03

    List your products

    Add each product with its unit, available quantity, minimum order and price. Selling needs a Markt seller account, which you can set up in the same step.

  4. 04

    Respond to buyers

    Answer enquiries and send offers on procurement requests from your business dashboard.
