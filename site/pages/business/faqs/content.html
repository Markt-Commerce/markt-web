General

Markt Business is the business-to-business side of Markt. It helps producers, manufacturers and suppliers find each other, share what they need, agree terms and coordinate orders, inside the Markt app and web app.

Buying and procurement

In business mode, search business listings by product, category, location and unit, or browse business profiles by type and industry. You can also post a procurement request and let suppliers come to you.

Open Procurement in business mode and create a request. Describe the product, and add the quantity and unit, specifications, a budget, delivery location, a needed-by date and photos if you have them. Suppliers respond with offers.

Yes. Sellers can mark a price as negotiable, and suppliers respond to requests with their own price. Agree the final terms in Markt chat before you place the order.

Payments and delivery

Orders placed on Markt are paid through Markt checkout, the same way as any Markt order. Agree the price and quantity with your supplier first, then place the order.

Markt delivery is available where it covers the route and the size of the load. For larger bulk loads, buyer and supplier agree transport between themselves, and you can keep those arrangements in the order's conversation.

Pricing

Setting up a business profile, listing products and posting procurement requests is free. Orders placed through Markt checkout carry the same fees as any other Markt order.
