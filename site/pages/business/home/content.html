Transactions and delivery

Agree first. Order when you're ready.

A procurement request is never an order on its own. Buyer and supplier agree specifications, quantity and price first; the order comes after, with every message about it in one place.

Payment

Orders placed on Markt are paid through Markt checkout, the same way as any Markt order.

Delivery

Markt delivery is available where it covers the route and load. Larger bulk loads are arranged between buyer and supplier.

Conversation

Chats stay linked to the product or request they're about, so the history is easy to find.

Due diligence

Check product specifications and samples with your supplier before you commit to large volumes.
