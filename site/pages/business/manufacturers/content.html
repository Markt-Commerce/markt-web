Register as a manufacturer How it works

Find raw materials

Search business listings by product, category, location and unit. Filter by how much is available and by minimum order.

Discover new suppliers

Browse business profiles by type and industry to widen your supplier base beyond the contacts you already have.

Submit procurement requirements

Describe what you need once. Suppliers who can meet it come to you.

Request quotations

Suppliers respond with a price and the quantity they can provide. Review the offers you receive, then accept or decline each one.

Communicate with producers

Discuss grades, samples and schedules in Markt chat, linked to the request or listing you're talking about.

Coordinate transactions and delivery

Once terms are agreed, place the order through Markt and track it from your business dashboard.

Procurement requests

Say exactly what you need.

A clear request gets better offers. Everything beyond the product and description is optional, so you can share as much detail as you have.

A request is not an order. You decide which supplier to work with, and an order is only created once you've both agreed the details.
