Deleting Your Account

Last updated: September 03, 2026

If you wish to delete your account on Markt, please follow the steps below. Deleting your account will remove your personal information from our platform, and you will no longer have access to your account or any associated data.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Log in to your Markt account.
  2. Navigate to the 'Settings' section.
  3. Click on 'Account Settings' and then select 'Delete Account'.
  4. Follow the prompts to confirm the deletion of your account.

Important Information

Please note that once your account is deleted, this action cannot be undone. All your data, including listings, messages, and transaction history, will be permanently removed from our system.

Contact Us

If you encounter any issues or have questions regarding the deletion of your account, please contact our support team at support@marktcommerce.com.

We value your privacy and are committed to ensuring that your personal information is handled securely and responsibly. You could also refer to our Privacy Policy for more information on how we manage your data.
