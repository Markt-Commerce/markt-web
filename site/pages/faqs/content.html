For Sellers

Simply download the app, create a profile, and apply for a seller account. Once verified, you can start listing products and hosting live sessions immediately.

Live selling allows you to broadcast video to your followers in real-time. You can showcase products, answer questions live, and viewers can purchase items instantly through an on-screen checkout button without leaving the stream.

We keep our fee structure simple: a flat percentage per successful transaction. There are no monthly subscription fees or listing costs to get started.

Security & Trust

We use industry-standard encryption for all data and never share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent.

Our 'Markt Guarantee' covers all purchases. If an item isn't shipped or doesn't arrive, we provide a full refund through our integrated dispute resolution system.

All sellers undergo a verification process that includes identity checks and, for certain categories, business documentation to ensure a safe environment for all users.
